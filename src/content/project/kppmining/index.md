## Description

KPP Mining is a business entity in the Astra group that provides integrated mining services (Integrated Mining Services) with a business portfolio including mining contractor services (Coal Mining Contractor), transportation services (Road & Hauling Services Operator), and port operator services (Port Operator Services).
